Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: game menu screen design concept, 1920x1080 (16:9), full-screen, no browser or OS chrome.

Primary request: design the "NEW CAMPAIGN" screen of a portable Total Annihilation engine called OPEN ANNIHILATION. It must match the visual style of the attached reference images exactly: the attached background (dark metallic sci-fi battlefield), the OPEN ANNIHILATION title graphic, the beveled gunmetal button images (normal, hover, disabled, pressed) and the attached Single Player submenu mockup, which is the screen this one is opened from. Same dark top and bottom vignette, same metal frames, same button shape and size (wide beveled 422x64 buttons), same typography.

Key design goal: the screen must support MANY factions, not only the two original ones (ARM and CORE), because mods can add new factions. The layout must scale to 2, 3, 5 or more factions without redesign.

Layout:
- Top: smaller OPEN ANNIHILATION title, under it a header "NEW CAMPAIGN" with thin metal rules on both sides (like the Single Player mockup header).
- Left column (about one third of width): a framed panel titled "CHOOSE FACTION" holding a vertical scrollable list of faction cards. Each card is a wide metal plate with a square emblem on the left, the faction name in large letters and one short line under it. Cards:
  1. "ARM" — subtitle "Arm Campaign · 25 missions", blue-green emblem; this card is SELECTED: brighter plate, warm glowing border, small triangle marker on the left edge.
  2. "CORE" — subtitle "Core Campaign · 25 missions", red emblem.
  3. "LEGION" — subtitle "Mod: Legion Campaign · 12 missions", purple emblem, with a tiny "MOD" tag plate on the card's right corner.
  4. a partially visible fourth card cut off by the panel edge, so it is clear the list scrolls; a slim metal scrollbar on the panel's right edge.
- Right area (about two thirds): a large framed detail panel for the selected faction:
  - top row: large ARM emblem, the name "ARM CAMPAIGN", and one line "The Arm — Defenders of freedom".
  - a short paragraph of story text (3 lines, placeholder-like but readable English sci-fi text about the war between Arm and Core).
  - a wide image slot showing a dark briefing-style planet picture.
  - a row "DIFFICULTY" with three small segment buttons "EASY", "NORMAL" (selected, glowing), "HARD".
  - a row "STARTING MISSION" with a small dropdown-like metal field showing "1 — New Hope".
- Bottom: a centered row of two of the standard wide buttons: "START CAMPAIGN" (hover state, warm glow) and "PREVIOUS MENU" with a small left-pointing arrow. First letter of every button label underlined (keyboard shortcut). Small version plate at bottom center below them, like in the Single Player mockup.

Colors: label text #ECE6C6, hover text #FFFADE, disabled text #969890, dark text outline #121410. Frames gunmetal with subtle bevels, warm amber glow for hover and selection.

Constraints: all on-screen text in English and spelled exactly as given; crisp readable lettering; no watermark; no real Total Annihilation logos — draw simple original geometric emblems; keep it a clean, readable UI layout mockup, not a painting.
