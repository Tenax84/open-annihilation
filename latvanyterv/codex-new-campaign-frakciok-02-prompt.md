# Codex prompt: New Campaign felület (new-campaign-frakciok-02.png)

Mintaképek: branding/main-menu/background.png, title.png, button-normal.png, button-hover.png, button-disabled.png, latvanyterv/single-player-almenu-01.png, és az eredeti játék New Campaign képernyőjéről készült kép.

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: game menu screen design concept, 16:9 (1920x1080), full-screen, no browser or OS chrome.

Primary request: design the "NEW CAMPAIGN" screen of a portable Total Annihilation engine called OPEN ANNIHILATION. Match the visual style of the attached reference images: the dark metallic sci-fi background, the OPEN ANNIHILATION title graphic, the beveled gunmetal wide buttons (normal, hover, disabled) and the attached Single Player submenu mockup, which opens this screen. Same top and bottom vignette, same metal frames, same button shape, same typography. The last attached image is a screenshot of the ORIGINAL game's New Campaign screen: keep its structure (choose a side by its emblem, then pick a campaign from a plain list box, difficulty, Previous Menu, Start), but restyle it in our look and make the side selection scale to many factions.

Key design goal: mods can add new factions, so the faction selector must hold 2, 3, 5 or more factions without redesign. Every faction can have SEVERAL campaigns, chosen from a visible list box with rows (NOT a dropdown or combobox).

Layout:
- Top: smaller OPEN ANNIHILATION title; under it a header "NEW CAMPAIGN" with thin metal rules on both sides, like the Single Player mockup.
- Upper band, full width: a metal strip labelled "SELECT SIDE" holding a horizontal row of square faction tiles, with a small left arrow button at the left end and a right arrow button at the right end of the strip (the row scrolls). Each tile: a dark green screen inside a bolted metal frame showing a glowing green wireframe-grid emblem (original simple geometric emblems, NOT the real Arm/Core logos), and a metal name plate under it.
  1. "ARM" — SELECTED: brighter glowing green screen, warm amber glowing frame, its name plate lit.
  2. "CORE".
  3. "LEGION" — with a tiny "MOD" tag plate on the tile's top right corner.
  4. a fourth tile cut off by the right end of the strip, so it is obvious more factions follow.
- Lower left (about 55 percent width): a label "SELECT CAMPAIGN:" above a large framed dark-green list box (like the original), rows of text in the game font, one campaign per row:
  "Arm Campaign" (selected row: highlighted bar, white text),
  "Arm Campaign - Arm Contingency",
  "Battle Tactics - Arm Long",
  "Battle Tactics - Arm Medium",
  "Battle Tactics - Arm Short",
  "Battle Tactics - Arm Very Short".
  A slim metal scrollbar on the list box's right edge.
- Lower right: a framed info panel for the selected campaign: title "ARM CAMPAIGN", a line "25 missions", and two short lines of story text in English about the war between Arm and Core. Under it a row "DIFFICULTY" with three small segment buttons "EASY", "MEDIUM" (selected, glowing), "HARD".
- Bottom: a centered row of two standard wide buttons: "PREVIOUS MENU" with a small left-pointing arrow, and "START" in hover state (warm glow). First letter of each button label underlined (keyboard shortcut). Small dark version plate at bottom center below them, like in the Single Player mockup.

Colors: label text #ECE6C6, hover text #FFFADE, disabled text #969890, dark text outline #121410. Frames gunmetal with subtle bevels and bolts; amber glow for hover and selection; green phosphor for emblem screens and the list box.

Constraints: all on-screen text in English, spelled exactly as given; crisp readable lettering; no watermark; no real Total Annihilation logos; a clean, readable UI layout mockup, not a painting. Style: gritty 1990s military sci-fi, metal, readable.
